🔬 Privacy Policy — Blood 10 Cells Puzzle

App: Applied Physiology Related to Blood Cells for MBBS Students — 10 Cells Identify + Function Puzzle
Developer: DR SM, Physiologist, Bangladesh
Effective date: October 19, 2026
Contact: via GitHub repo issues / Render service contact

1. Overview

This is an educational quiz web app. It runs fully in your browser. We do not require accounts, logins, or sign-ups. Quiz scores are kept only in your device's temporary page memory and are lost on refresh — we do not store them on any server.

2. Data We Collect

  • No personal data collected by us. We do not ask for name, email, phone, location, or device identifiers.
  • No quiz answers sent to us. All scoring happens locally in your browser via JavaScript.
  • Images (real1–real10.webp, 10cells.jpg) are static educational smear images served from our host (Render CDN). Loading them sends only standard web-log data (IP, user-agent) to the host, as with any website.

3. Third-Party Services (Ads / Analytics)

Current version shows only placeholder in-app promo cards (no third-party ad SDK). If Google AdSense / AdMob or analytics is added later, those providers may collect device/advertising identifiers and usage data under their own policies:

  • Google AdSense / AdMob: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from ads
  • Render (hosting/CDN): Render Privacy Policy

You can limit ad personalization in your Google account / device ad settings.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

  • We do not set tracking cookies.
  • The service worker (sw.js/worker.js) caches quiz files for offline use via the Cache API. Clear site data in your browser to remove it.
  • No cross-site tracking.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is an MBBS-level medical education tool intended for adult learners. It is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no children's data. Medical content is educational only, not medical advice.

6. Medical Disclaimer

Content (WBC/RBC morphology, Guyton/Ganong/Hoffbrand references) is for study only and does not replace textbooks, microscopy training, or clinical judgment. Always confirm smear findings with your instructor/pathologist.

7. Data Security & Retention

Since we collect no personal data, there is nothing to retain or share. Host access logs (if any) are managed by Render under its policy. We never sell data.

8. Your Rights

Nothing to delete or export on our side. To remove cached files, clear your browser's site data. For questions contact us via the repo / hosting contact page.

9. Changes

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes. Continued use after changes means acceptance.
